◆BlaiseVR

23 lifelike models — the human body, engines, okadas, danfos, volcanoes, planets and Nigerian landmarks — built from a quarter-million lit particles and controlled by your hands.

🫰Snap to summonSnap your fingers and the particles appear. Make a fist to form the model.
✋Open to explodeThe wider you open your hand, the further every part flies apart.
☝️Point to learnPoint at a part to select it. Pinch 🤏 to pull it out. Thumbs-up 👍 to hear it explained.
✌️Twist, tilt, zoomTwist your hand to rotate, raise it to tilt, two hands 🙌 to zoom. Peace sign for the next model.

Hand tracking runs on this device. Video never leaves your computer. Also works with mouse, touch, keyboard and voice. Drop any .glb, .obj, .stl, image or .json to make your own.
